Screaming Frog vs Semrush

Screaming Frog is a desktop crawler sold as an annual licence. Semrush is a subscription research suite that also audits sites. They overlap on technical checks, but they solve different jobs and are rarely direct substitutes.

The short answer

Our verdict

Screaming Frog is the better tool for a deep technical audit you run yourself: it is configurable and sold as a flat annual licence. Semrush is the better tool when audits must sit beside keyword, backlink and competitor research, with scheduled crawls and reporting in a shared workspace.

If you only need to crawl a small site, Screaming Frog's free 500-URL tier may be sufficient and neither paid plan is necessary.

Choose Screaming Frog if…

The job is a deep technical crawl that you run yourself, on sites you can reach from your machine.

  • You need a full technical crawl without a monthly subscription
  • You want custom extraction, JavaScript rendering and log-file analysis
  • You work on large or messy sites where crawl control matters
  • You are comfortable installing and configuring desktop software

Choose Semrush if…

You need research data plus scheduled audits, reporting and a shared workspace.

  • You need keyword, backlink and competitor research alongside audits
  • You want scheduled crawls and reports without running a desktop app
  • You manage client sites and need shared access and reporting
  • You will use the wider marketing dataset

Who should skip each product — and who should choose neither

Skip Screaming Frog if…

  • You need keyword, backlink or competitor research
  • You need scheduled cloud crawls your team can open anywhere
  • You cannot install desktop software on your machine
  • You need an all-in-one marketing dashboard for clients

Skip Semrush if…

  • Your only job is a technical crawl of a site you own
  • You need raw crawl depth, custom extraction or log-file analysis
  • You want a one-off annual cost rather than a subscription
  • Your budget cannot support a recurring marketing-suite price

Choose neither when

Choose neither if a free crawl covers your site and you do not need research data. If you need backlink analysis more than crawling, compare Ahrefs. If you need an all-in-one suite with paid-search research, Semrush remains relevant, but Moz Pro may be enough for a single site.

How to decide: buyer criteria that matter

Ignore the feature list on the pricing page until you have answered these questions. They decide which plan you actually need.

Desktop or cloud

Decide whether the crawl must run on your machine or be scheduled and shared by a team.

Crawl depth

Check JavaScript rendering, custom extraction, log-file analysis and how many URLs you must process.

Research needs

A crawler does not replace a keyword or backlink database. List the data you need before comparing price.

Licence model

Compare a flat annual licence with a recurring subscription and model the second year, not just the first.

Team access

A desktop licence is not a shared cloud workspace. Count the people who need access.

Reporting

Decide whether you need raw exports for analysis or client-ready reports.

Genuine limitations

Every tool is a compromise. These are the drawbacks we would want to know before paying.

Screaming Frog limitations

  • Desktop software with no cloud workspace or built-in rank tracking
  • The free tier stops at 500 URLs
  • Configuration takes time and technical knowledge
  • Large crawls depend on your machine's memory and connection

Semrush limitations

  • Subscription pricing at every tier
  • Audit crawl limits depend on your plan
  • Less raw crawl control than a dedicated desktop crawler
  • The interface is broad and takes time to learn

Switching, export and migration

These tools store different things. Screaming Frog keeps crawl output on your machine; Semrush keeps project history in your account. Export what you need before cancelling either one.

Moving from Semrush to Screaming Frog

  • Export crawl data, issue lists and custom extraction results as CSV before you stop using the licence.
  • Recreate the audit scope in Semrush; crawl configuration does not import.
  • Seed the new audit with the same URL list so issue counts are comparable.
  • Expect different issue classifications and scoring between the two tools.

Moving from Screaming Frog to Semrush

  • Export audit issues and URL lists from Semrush as CSV.
  • Use the exported URLs as the seed list for a Screaming Frog crawl.
  • Rebuild custom extraction and log-file checks; these have no Semrush equivalent.
  • Keep Semrush active for historical trend comparison during the first month.
Plan for overlap. Many technical SEOs run both: Screaming Frog for the deep crawl and Semrush for research, monitoring and client reporting. That is a legitimate outcome, not a failure to choose.

Use-case conclusion

Technical audit of a large site

Screaming Frog — deeper crawl control and a flat annual cost.

Keyword and competitor research

Semrush — the crawler has no research database.

Scheduled client audits

Semrush — cloud scheduling and reporting are built in.

JavaScript-heavy site with custom extraction

Screaming Frog — configuration is the point of the tool.

One-off crawl under 500 URLs

Screaming Frog free tier — no paid plan needed.

Technical plus research plus reporting

Both — budget for two tools rather than forcing one to do everything.

Alternatives worth considering

If you need one tool rather than two, decide which half of the job you can compromise on. A suite can cover basic audits; a crawler cannot replace research data.

Frequently asked questions

Is Screaming Frog cheaper than Semrush?

Screaming Frog is €245 per licence per year for 1–4 licences, while Semrush SEO is $139 per month, or $117.33 billed yearly. They are different currencies and different products, so the comparison only matters after you decide whether you need research data as well as a crawler.

Can Screaming Frog replace Semrush?

No. Screaming Frog crawls sites; it does not provide keyword, backlink or competitor research. It can replace Semrush's audit for a technical specialist, but not the rest of the suite.

Does Semrush have a free plan?

Semrush offers a limited free account with daily caps and a 7-day trial on paid plans. Screaming Frog's free tier is a real crawl capped at 500 URLs, which makes it more useful for a small technical audit.

Do I need both tools?

If you do technical SEO and also need keyword, backlink and competitor research, yes, many teams run both. Buy the crawler for depth and the suite for data and reporting rather than expecting one subscription to do both well.

Is Screaming Frog hard to learn?

The basics are approachable, but custom extraction, JavaScript rendering and log-file analysis assume technical SEO knowledge. Budget time for configuration before a large crawl.
