Moz Pro vs Screaming Frog

Moz Pro is a subscription that covers several jobs adequately. Screaming Frog is a single job done deeply. If you need one invoice and a monthly dashboard, Moz Pro wins; if you need the best possible crawl, Screaming Frog wins.

The short answer

Our verdict

Moz Pro's crawl is good enough for routine hygiene on a normal site. Screaming Frog is better the moment crawl depth, rendering or custom extraction matters. The deciding question is not which crawler is stronger, but whether you also need the keyword and rank-tracking jobs Moz Pro bundles.

Choose Moz Pro if…

You want one subscription that answers 'what should we target?' and 'is the site healthy?' without stitching tools together.

  • You need keyword research and rank tracking as well as a crawl
  • You prefer a cloud dashboard over local exports
  • You want one subscription and one interface
  • Your site is small enough for plan crawl limits

Choose Screaming Frog if…

You already know what to target, and the remaining work is technical: migrations, redirects, rendering and large-scale QA.

  • Technical SEO is the main job, not a monthly checkbox
  • You need unlimited crawl size, custom extraction or log analysis
  • You work with developers and need exportable evidence
  • You already have keyword and rank data elsewhere

Which workflow fits you

Moz Pro is the pragmatic choice for a small marketing team. One login covers keyword ideas, ranking trends, a crawl and a basic link view. The crawl is not as configurable as a desktop tool, and campaign limits apply, but it is enough to catch broken links, duplicate titles, missing canonicals and slow pages on a site of a few thousand URLs.

Screaming Frog becomes necessary when the crawl itself is the deliverable. That includes migrations where you must compare staging and production, sites where content or links only exist after JavaScript runs, and audits that require extracting specific elements from thousands of templates. A paid licence also removes the 500-URL ceiling that makes the free version unsuitable for larger sites.

Cost is not the deciding factor in the way it first appears. Moz Pro costs roughly six times a Screaming Frog licence per year at the Standard tier. But Moz Pro also replaces a keyword tool and a rank tracker. If you drop Moz Pro for Screaming Frog, you still need to pay for those jobs. The fair comparison is Moz Pro versus Screaming Frog plus a keyword/rank subscription.

A common and sensible arrangement is Moz Pro for ongoing campaigns and Screaming Frog's free tier for occasional deep checks. Upgrade the crawler only when a specific project exceeds 500 URLs or needs rendering and extraction. That keeps the subscription count low without pretending a cloud audit is a full technical crawler.

Frequently asked questions

Is Screaming Frog included in Moz Pro?

No. Moz Pro includes its own cloud Site Crawl, which is a different tool with plan limits. Screaming Frog is a separate desktop application with its own licence.

Which crawler finds more issues?

Screaming Frog generally exposes more technical detail because it is configurable and runs locally. Moz Pro's crawl is simpler and aimed at routine monitoring rather than deep technical forensics.

Can I use Screaming Frog for free?

Yes, up to 500 URLs per crawl, with advanced configuration and features restricted. That is enough for small sites and for evaluating whether a licence is worth it.

Which is better value?

For a solo site owner who needs research, rankings and a crawl, Moz Pro bundles more jobs. For a technical specialist who already has research data, Screaming Frog is cheaper and deeper.
