ChatGPT vs Claude

Both are strong general assistants, so the real choice is price, limits and the extras around the chat window. Here is how the official plans compare, and who each one suits.

The short verdict

Quick verdict

Pick ChatGPT if you want image creation and custom GPTs, or a lower-cost paid plan. Pick Claude if your work centres on long documents, writing and code, and you want its document-style outputs and Claude Code under one subscription. If you are unsure, both have a free plan, so run the same real task in each.

ToolNest did not run benchmarks or hands-on tests for this page. The comparison rests on what OpenAI and Anthropic say officially about their plans, so it tells you what each plan is documented to include, not which one gives better answers on your work.

Which one should you choose?

Choose ChatGPT if...

  • You want a paid plan for less than $20 a month. Go is $8 a month in the US and raises limits for messages, uploads and image creation, as long as you can live with possible ads and check it is offered in your region.
  • You want image creation and custom GPTs in the same app as chat. Both appear in OpenAI's plan descriptions, and Anthropic's pricing page does not list image creation.
  • You expect to outgrow a single plan and want steps to grow into. ChatGPT Pro offers three usage tiers above Plus (check current prices on OpenAI's pricing page), and Business starts at 2 users.
  • You use deep research and want to try it first. Free includes limited deep research, and Plus lists expanded deep research.

Choose Claude if...

  • You regularly work with very long documents, large codebases or long conversations and want the largest documented context window. Anthropic says its newest paid-plan models reach 1M tokens.
  • You write or review code and want the same subscription to cover chat and Claude Code. Claude Code is included from Pro, and usage is shared across claude.ai, Claude Code and Desktop.
  • You want finished outputs such as Artifacts, documents, slides and designs, even on the Free plan, where Anthropic lists Claude Design, Slides and Docs.
  • You work in Microsoft 365 or Chrome all day. Anthropic lists Claude in Chrome and Microsoft 365 as Pro features, and Microsoft 365 on Team.

Trade-offs to know about

ChatGPT limitations

  • Limits vary by feature and plan, and OpenAI says limits apply even on paid tiers. Check the plan table on OpenAI's pricing page before relying on any one capability.
  • Go may show ads, and Go is not available in every region.
  • Context windows differ by plan and model type. Free is documented at 27K tokens for Instant models, which is small for long documents.
  • Pro comes in three usage tiers, and we could not confirm their consumer prices from ChatGPT's main pricing page, so check the current price before planning around it.

Claude limitations

  • Usage is rationed by a 5-hour window and a weekly limit, and long conversations, big attachments and heavy features use more of it. Anthropic may change limits to manage capacity.
  • Free does not include Opus or Fable, and on Pro, Fable is not in the plan limits and runs on pay-as-you-go usage credits.
  • There is no plan between Free and Pro, so the entry price is $17 to $20 a month.
  • Anthropic's pricing page does not list image creation as a plan feature, so confirm this in the app if it matters to you.

Frequently asked questions

Slightly, if you pay annually. Anthropic lists Claude Pro at $17 a month billed annually ($200 up front) or $20 a month billed monthly. OpenAI's documentation lists ChatGPT Plus at $20 a month. ChatGPT also has a cheaper Go plan at $8 a month in the US, which Claude has no direct equivalent for: Claude's next step up from Free is Pro. Prices can vary by region and tax.

Anthropic says its newest models on paid plans support context windows of up to 1M tokens, while others support 500K or 200K. OpenAI's pricing page lists context by plan and model type, for example 54K tokens for Instant models and 256K for reasoning on Plus, and 128K and 400K on Pro. These figures are not like-for-like because they depend on model and mode, and ToolNest has not benchmarked either. Test both on a real document of your own before paying.

You can learn a lot from them, but they are limited in different ways. ChatGPT Free offers unlimited text chats subject to abuse guardrails, with limited messages for uploads, image creation, voice, deep research and memory. Claude Free has limited usage every 5 hours and every week, and does not include Opus or Fable models. Run the same two or three real tasks in both, then check whether the paid plan you would need fits your budget.
