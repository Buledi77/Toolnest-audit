Why Hostinger fits this goal

Hostinger is a web hosting company. You can use it to host a website, build one with its website builder, register a domain and set up business email from one account. It offers shared, WordPress, cloud and VPS hosting, so you can start small and move up later.

  • Hosting, domain and site builder in one account
  • Beginner-friendly plans
  • Free SSL listed on its plans

How to get started

  1. Decide what the site is for: a portfolio, a blog, a business page or a small shop.
  2. Choose a plan, pick a domain name and open the website builder or install WordPress.
  3. Build a few clear pages: what you do, who it is for, and how to contact you.
  4. Connect business email on your domain, then check the site on a phone before you share it.
