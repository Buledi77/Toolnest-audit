Why Descript fits this goal

Descript turns your recording into a transcript, and you edit the audio or video by editing the words. Delete a sentence in the text and it disappears from the recording, which makes editing feel like writing.

  • Edit by editing the text
  • Automatic transcripts
  • Removes filler words fast

How to get started

  1. Record or import your audio or video and let the tool create the transcript.
  2. Delete mistakes, repeated takes and filler words directly in the text.
  3. Clean the sound with the noise-removal option if your room is noisy.
  4. Export the result, or send it to a full editor if you need visual effects.
