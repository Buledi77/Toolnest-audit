Best Zapier alternatives, by cost and control

Zapier is the safest choice for business integrations, which is why most teams do not leave until the bill grows. The alternatives below are cheaper at volume, more visual, or self-hosted. Each one gives up some of Zapier's polish, app coverage or support.

Why people look for Zapier alternatives

Task-based pricing multiplies with workflow length. A five-step Zap can consume up to five tasks per run, so high-volume automations become expensive.

There is no self-hosted option. Teams with data-residency requirements cannot keep execution data on their own infrastructure.

Simple trigger-action recipes are overpriced on Zapier compared with IFTTT, especially for personal or smart-home use.

Some advanced controls and support features are reserved for higher task tiers, which raises the real cost of a small team deployment.

The shortlist: best Zapier alternatives

Prices were checked in November 2026. Zapier and Make figures are annual-billed monthly equivalents in USD; n8n Cloud is priced in EUR. Confirm current tiers and limits before buying.

1

Make

Automation

The best hosted alternative for cost and visual branching.

What it does better: Routers, iterators, error handlers and a visual canvas at a much lower cost per operation.

Trade-off: A steeper learning curve and fewer integrations. Non-technical staff need more support.

Price context: Free 1,000 credits/mo; Core $9/mo billed annually for 10,000 credits.

2

n8n

Automation

For technical teams that want execution-based pricing and control.

What it does better: Unlimited steps per execution, code nodes, HTTP requests and a free self-hosted Community Edition.

Trade-off: You maintain the infrastructure, or pay for n8n Cloud. App coverage requires more custom work.

Price context: Community Edition free self-hosted; Cloud from €20/mo billed annually.

3

IFTTT

Automation

For simple personal recipes where Zapier is overkill.

What it does better: A very cheap entry price, 1,000+ services and unlimited Applets on the free tier.

Trade-off: Limited branching, slower polling and weaker business app coverage.

Price context: Free 1,000 credits/mo; Starter $2.09/mo billed annually.

What you give up when you switch

The hidden cost of leaving Zapier is app coverage and operational confidence. Make and n8n can usually rebuild a workflow, but not always with a native connector, and not always without engineering time. Before switching, list every app in your busiest Zaps and confirm it exists on the alternative. A cheaper plan with two missing integrations is not a saving.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest Zapier alternative?

IFTTT is cheapest for simple recipes, and Make is cheapest for hosted multi-step workflows at $9 per month billed annually. Self-hosted n8n has no licence fee but adds infrastructure and maintenance costs.

Can Make replace Zapier?

For most multi-step workflows, yes. The main gaps are app coverage and ease of use for non-technical staff. Make's canvas is more powerful but less guided.

Is n8n a good Zapier replacement?

For technical teams, often. It offers execution-based pricing and self-hosting, but requires more setup and maintenance than Zapier.

Does Zapier have a free plan?

Yes, with 100 tasks per month, two-step Zaps and 15-minute polling. It is enough to evaluate the platform, not to run a business process.
