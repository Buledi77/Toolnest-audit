Best beehiiv alternatives for creators and publishers

beehiiv combines newsletter publishing, audience growth and monetization. If you use only part of that bundle, a narrower tool can be a better fit. These are the three alternatives we have reviewed in full, matched to the job that usually causes the switch.

Why people look for beehiiv alternatives

beehiiv is built around publishing, growth and monetization: the newsletter, recommendation-style audience growth and revenue products such as ads, boosts and paid subscriptions. That bundle is valuable if you use it. If you mainly need email automation, a landing page and a product checkout, you may be paying for a publishing network you do not need.

The second common reason is automation depth. Creator businesses often need welcome sequences, tag-based journeys and a simple digital-product funnel. A creator-focused email platform can be a closer fit than a publishing platform, especially when the free tier is generous.

The third is scope. If the newsletter is one channel in a wider marketing stack, a general platform with campaigns, e-commerce integrations and audience management may be easier to consolidate. If you need SMS, transactional email or a CRM view, a multi-channel platform is the better shape.

Before switching, separate the tool from the audience. Your subscriber list, paid subscriptions, archive URLs and advertiser relationships are the assets. The platform is replaceable; the migration is the risk.

The shortlist: best beehiiv alternatives

Prices are published entry points read in November 2026. Newsletter pricing usually scales with subscriber count, so compare the tier for your actual list, not the headline.

1

Kit

Email Marketing

The closest creator-focused swap. Kit is built around email lists, visual automations, landing pages and digital products rather than a publishing and ad network.

What it does better: Visual automations, unlimited broadcasts and forms on the free plan, a free tier up to 10,000 subscribers, and digital-product checkout in the same account.

Trade-off: Less of a publishing and monetization network. You bring your own audience growth and ad deals, and the free plan excludes automations.

Price context: Free up to 10,000 subscribers; Creator $33/mo ($390/year) for 1,000 subscribers; Pro $50/mo first year ($593 first year). Prices rise with list size.

Mailchimp

Email Marketing

A broader marketing platform rather than a newsletter-first tool. It fits when email is one part of a larger campaign and e-commerce stack.

What it does better: Campaign building, audience management, automations and a wide integration directory, with a free plan available for a small list.

Trade-off: More general-purpose, and pricing scales with contacts and send volume. You may pay for modules a pure newsletter does not need.

Price context: Free plan available; paid plans scale by contact count and email volume. Check the current tier for your list size.

3

Brevo

Email Marketing

A multi-channel platform for email, SMS and transactional messages, with CRM features. It fits teams that need more than a newsletter.

What it does better: Email plus SMS and WhatsApp, transactional email, and a free plan that allows up to 300 emails per day.

Trade-off: The interface and feature set are broader than a newsletter tool, and advanced automation or higher volume may require a paid tier.

Price context: Free plan available; paid plans scale with email volume and contacts. Check the current Starter and Standard pricing for your list.

What you give up when you switch

The switch is rarely just a login change. Export subscribers and tags, confirm custom fields, rebuild forms and landing pages, recreate automations, and decide what happens to paid subscriptions and payment processing. Newsletter archive URLs may need redirects if they are indexed. Keep the old account active until the new list is verified, and warm up sending gradually. The highest-risk item is paid subscriptions: confirm how existing payments and subscriber access will move before you cancel.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kit a good beehiiv alternative?

Yes, if your priority is creator automations, landing pages and digital products. It is a weaker fit if you rely on beehiiv's publishing and monetization network. Compare the free tier and the paid automation features against your actual workflow.

Which alternative is cheapest?

It depends on list size and volume. Kit's free plan supports up to 10,000 subscribers; Brevo's free plan allows up to 300 emails per day; Mailchimp also has a free plan. Compare the tier for your real list, not the headline.

Can I move my paid subscribers?

Treat this as the highest-risk part of the migration. Confirm with both platforms how paid subscriptions, payment processing and subscriber access will transfer before you cancel. Do not assume the export includes billing.

Will I lose my newsletter archive?

The content may be exportable, but the public archive URLs belong to the old platform. If those pages have search traffic or backlinks, plan redirects or rebuild the archive on the new site.

Do I need Mailchimp if I only send a newsletter?

No. Mailchimp is the better fit when email is part of a wider marketing stack with e-commerce and campaign needs. A newsletter-first tool is usually simpler and cheaper for a single publication.

What about Substack?

Substack is a different model: a hosted publishing and subscription marketplace with its own audience and fee structure. We have not reviewed it in full, so research its current terms directly rather than treating it as a like-for-like swap.
