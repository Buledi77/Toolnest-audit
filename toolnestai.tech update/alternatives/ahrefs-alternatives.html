Alternatives by use case

Best Ahrefs alternatives, and when each one makes sense

Ahrefs is excellent at what it does, which is why people seldom leave over data quality. The usual reasons are cost, credit limits, a need for broader marketing data, or a technical crawl that goes beyond a cloud audit. Each alternative below solves one of those problems, and each has a cost of its own.

Why people look for Ahrefs alternatives

Ahrefs is expensive for a single-site workflow, and research consumes credits quickly. A smaller team may not use enough of the dataset to justify Lite or Standard.

It is not a paid-search or local-marketing platform. If you need PPC research and client reporting across channels, Semrush covers more ground.

The interface is dense. Non-specialists often prefer Moz Pro's campaign structure, even though the data is shallower.

Site Audit is convenient but not as configurable as a desktop crawler for JavaScript rendering, custom extraction and large migrations.

The shortlist: best Ahrefs alternatives

Prices were checked in November 2026. Ahrefs entry prices vary with billing period. Surfer SEO's plans were not re-verified for this page, so no figure is quoted.

1

Semrush

SEO

The broadest alternative when organic search is only part of the job.

What it does better: Paid-search research, local SEO, content tooling and multi-project reporting sit alongside organic keyword and backlink data.

Trade-off: Higher entry price and more menus. Some datasets are broad rather than best-in-class.

Price context: SEO from $139/mo, or $117.33/mo billed yearly; limited free account.

2

Moz Pro

SEO

The lower-cost option for rank tracking and routine audits.

What it does better: A simpler campaign workflow and a lower entry price, with keyword research, rank tracking and crawl included.

Trade-off: Smaller backlink index and weaker content research. Not a like-for-like data replacement.

Price context: Standard $99/mo, or $79/mo billed yearly.

3

Screaming Frog

SEO

For the technical half of the job, at a fraction of the cost.

What it does better: Unlimited crawl size, configurable JavaScript rendering, custom extraction and migration comparisons.

Trade-off: No keyword, backlink or rank data at all. You still need a research source.

Price context: £199 per licence per year; free version crawls 500 URLs.

Surfer SEO

SEO

If the gap is content production rather than research data.

What it does better: Content outlines, on-page scoring and SERP-based suggestions in an editor built for writers.

Trade-off: Narrow scope. It does not replace link research, rank tracking or a full audit.

Price context: Paid plans with a trial; check current pricing on Surfer's site.

What you give up when you switch

The hardest part of leaving Ahrefs is replacing the backlink index. Moz Pro and Semrush both have link data, but neither is identical, and coverage varies by niche. Before cancelling, export the competitor domains and link prospects you rely on, then check whether the alternative surfaces the same referring domains. A cheaper plan that misses half your prospects is not cheaper.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest Ahrefs alternative?

Moz Pro Standard at $99 per month is the cheapest full suite among the common alternatives, and Screaming Frog's free tier covers small technical audits. Ahrefs' own Starter plan is cheaper still, but it is limited.

Is Semrush a good replacement for Ahrefs?

For teams that need broader marketing data, yes. For pure backlink and content research, Ahrefs is usually the stronger dataset, so the switch is a trade rather than a clear upgrade.

Can I replace Ahrefs with free tools?

For a very small site, free keyword tools plus Screaming Frog's free crawl can cover the basics. They will not replace large-scale link-gap research or ongoing rank tracking.

Does Moz Pro have backlink data?

Yes, through Link Explorer, but the index is smaller than Ahrefs'. It is adequate for many niches and not a full substitute for link-led prospecting.
