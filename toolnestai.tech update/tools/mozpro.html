Moz Pro review: a focused SEO suite at a mid-market price

Moz Pro bundles keyword research, rank tracking, site crawling and link data into one subscription. It is the least overwhelming of the big SEO suites, but it is not the deepest. Here is where it earns its $99 entry price and where it does not.

Moz Pro overview

Moz Pro is an all-in-one SEO workspace for people who manage a site rather than research the whole market. You add a domain, choose keywords, run a crawl and work through a prioritised list of issues. The interface is campaign-based, so the next action is usually obvious even if you are not a full-time SEO.

Why it stands out

The combination of rank tracking, site crawl and keyword research in one place is genuinely useful for a solo marketer or small team, and the entry price is lower than the equivalent tier at Semrush or Ahrefs.

Who Moz Pro is best for, and who should skip it

Best for

  • Solo site owners and small marketing teams who need one clear SEO dashboard
  • Rank tracking across a defined keyword set, with history you can show a client
  • Routine site crawls to catch broken links, duplicate titles and missing canonicals
  • Keyword research where a simpler difficulty score is enough
  • Anyone who wants Moz's guides and community alongside the tool

Skip it if

  • You need large-scale backlink or content research; Ahrefs is stronger
  • You need paid-search, local-listing or broad content-marketing modules; Semrush is broader
  • You are auditing a very large or JavaScript-heavy site; a dedicated crawler goes deeper
  • You need unlimited crawl size or custom extraction on a low budget

Moz Pro pros and cons

Pros

  • The campaign model is easy to understand and hard to get lost in
  • Rank tracking, site crawl and keyword research share one interface
  • Keyword difficulty and SERP analysis are presented clearly for non-specialists
  • Site Crawl tracks issues over time rather than only producing a one-off export
  • Link Explorer is adequate for many niches and simpler than enterprise link tools
  • Yearly billing lowers the effective monthly price, and entry costs less than the big two suites

Cons

  • The backlink index is smaller than Ahrefs' or Semrush's, so link-gap work can miss domains
  • No serious paid-search or PPC research workflow
  • Content tooling is limited compared with Semrush's content toolkit
  • Plan limits on keyword queries, tracked rankings, crawl pages and rows are easy to hit
  • The cloud crawler is less configurable than a desktop tool such as Screaming Frog
  • Reporting is clean but less customisable than agency-focused platforms

Features that matter in practice

Keyword research

Keyword Explorer groups keywords, shows difficulty and surfaces SERP features, with plan-based query limits.

Rank tracking

Daily ranking history for a defined keyword set, tied to a campaign rather than a separate product.

Site crawl

Scheduled crawls flag broken links, redirects, duplicate metadata, canonical problems and other on-site issues.

Link research

Link Explorer shows referring domains, anchor text and competitor links within the plan's row limits.

Page optimisation

On-page suggestions for a target keyword, useful as a second opinion rather than an automated writer.

Reporting

Campaign dashboards summarise visibility and crawl health for clients or stakeholders.

Free plan and trial

Moz Pro does not advertise a permanent free plan. It offers a trial so you can test the campaign workflow; confirm the current trial length and terms on Moz's site. The trial is enough to run a crawl and a small keyword set, not to evaluate a year of client work.

Frequently asked questions

Is Moz Pro good for beginners?

Yes. The campaign structure, plain-language difficulty scores and built-in crawl make it easier to learn than Semrush or Ahrefs. You still need to understand what a canonical or a redirect is before the crawl findings become useful.

How much does Moz Pro cost?

Standard is $99 per month, or $79 per month billed yearly. Medium is $179 per month ($143 yearly) and Large is $299 per month ($239 yearly). Prices are USD list prices checked in November 2026.

Does Moz Pro include keyword research?

Yes. Keyword Explorer is part of the platform, with query and row limits that depend on your plan. It is a credible research tool, though smaller in scope than Ahrefs or Semrush.

Is Moz Pro better than Ahrefs?

For a simpler, cheaper rank-tracking and audit workflow, often yes. For backlink and content research, Ahrefs is stronger. The right choice depends on whether you need data depth or a lower-cost daily workspace.
