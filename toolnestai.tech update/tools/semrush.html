Semrush overview

Semrush is an all-in-one SEO and marketing toolkit. You can research keywords, see what your competitors rank for, audit your site and track your own rankings from one account.

Why we recommend it

It answers the first SEO question, what should I write about, with real search data and shows what competitors already rank for.

Who Semrush is best for, and who should skip it

Best for

  • Bloggers and site owners who want to find topics people search for
  • Freelancers and agencies doing competitor research for clients
  • Anyone who wants many SEO tools under one login

Not best for

  • People who only need an occasional keyword idea
  • Absolute beginners who will not use most of the tools
  • Anyone not building a site or content that relies on search

Semrush pros and cons

Pros

  • Deep keyword research
  • Shows what rivals rank for
  • Many SEO tools in one

Cons

  • A lot of features, so it takes time to learn
  • Paid plans cost more than basic keyword tools
  • Search data is an estimate, not an exact count
