ElevenLabs overview

ElevenLabs turns written text into natural-sounding speech. You can narrate a video, a course or an audiobook without recording yourself, and many languages and voice styles are available.

Why we recommend it

It is the quickest route from a written script to a believable voiceover, and it saves re-recording when you change a line.

Who ElevenLabs is best for, and who should skip it

Best for

  • Narrated videos and course lessons built from a script, where changing one line means regenerating a clip instead of re-recording
  • Teams that need the same audio in several languages; Eleven v4 and dubbing cover 90+ languages
  • A consistent presenter or brand voice, using Professional Voice Cloning to train a model on your own audio
  • Developers adding speech to an app, with Flash v2.5 for low latency and Eleven v4 for expressiveness

Skip it if

  • You need commercial rights on a $0 budget: the Free plan does not include them
  • You want a close copy of a distinctive accent: Instant Voice Cloning may not capture it, and Professional Voice Cloning is only available on Creator and above
  • You plan to clone someone else's voice: Professional Voice Cloning only accepts your own verified voice, even with that person's permission
  • You plan heavy dubbing or sound design: every product draws on the same monthly credits, and dubbing consumes them quickly
  • You also need to cut picture: ElevenLabs outputs audio only, so you still need a video editor such as Descript

ElevenLabs pros and cons

Pros

  • Expressive speech with a model for each job: Eleven v4 for quality and 90+ languages, Flash v2.5 for low latency and 40,000-character requests
  • Instant Voice Cloning on Starter is built for 1–2 minutes of audio, with at least 10 seconds in total, and is usable almost immediately
  • Professional Voice Cloning on Creator and above trains a dedicated model on your own voice for higher fidelity than an instant clone
  • One credit pool covers text to speech, dubbing, sound effects, music and speech to text, so there is a single monthly allowance to track
  • You keep ownership of the audio you generate

Cons

  • Free is a limited allowance: 10,000 credits a month, about 10,000 characters of standard text to speech
  • Credits drain quickly on other products: dubbing costs 2,000–10,000 credits per minute, speech to text 330 per minute and sound effects 200 per generation (see ElevenLabs pricing)
  • Instant clones are an approximation rather than a trained model, and the accent cannot be changed after creation; Professional Voice Cloning recommends 30–180 minutes of audio and typically takes 3–6 hours to fine-tune
  • Quality and throughput are tiered: 192 kbps audio and 44.1 kHz PCM API output start at Pro ($99), and concurrent requests run from 2 on Free to 10 on Pro
  • Per-request limits vary by model (10,000 characters on v4 and Multilingual v2, 40,000 on Flash v2.5), and output is nondeterministic, so long scripts need splitting and repeat takes will not be identical

ElevenLabs pricing and the free plan

ElevenLabs bills monthly, and each plan includes a credit allowance shared across text to speech, dubbing, sound effects and its other tools. Text to speech uses about 1 credit per character, so long scripts use credits quickly. Annual billing costs ten months for twelve.
