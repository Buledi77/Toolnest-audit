Hostinger overview

Hostinger is a web hosting company. You can use it to host a website, build one with its website builder, register a domain and set up business email from one account. It offers shared, WordPress, cloud and VPS hosting, so you can start small and move up later.

Why we recommend it

It puts hosting, a domain and a website builder in one beginner-friendly account, which is often all a first website needs.

Free domain, money-back guarantee and migration

  • 30-day money-back guarantee on hosting plans, subject to the official terms
  • Free domain for the first year on eligible plans; the domain renews at its standard rate
  • Free website migration on eligible plans
  • No permanent free hosting tier is advertised; the money-back window is the evaluation route

Read the refund exclusions before buying. Domains, add-ons and some services may not be refundable, and the guarantee is time-limited from the purchase date.

Who Hostinger is best for, and who should skip it

Best for

  • Beginners launching a first website, portfolio or blog
  • Freelancers who want their own site and their own domain
  • Small businesses that need hosting and business email in one place

Not best for

  • People who want a sales funnel tool with nothing to host or maintain
  • Large sites that need a dedicated infrastructure team
  • Anyone who does not want to manage a website at all

Who should choose neither

Choose a managed website builder instead if…

You do not want to manage hosting, updates, plugins or backups. A fully managed site builder takes those tasks away, usually for a higher monthly price.

Choose a specialist platform instead if…

You only need a sales funnel, an online course or a storefront with hosted checkout. Those products remove the hosting decision rather than asking you to run it.

Hostinger is also a poor fit if you need a dedicated infrastructure team, custom networking or compliance work that a shared plan cannot support. In that case, compare managed cloud or dedicated hosting rather than a beginner plan.

Switching to Hostinger

Moving a site is mostly preparation, not the transfer itself. Hostinger offers free migration on eligible plans, but you still need to check what moves and what breaks.

  • Back up your files, database and email before changing any nameservers
  • Confirm which migration service your plan includes and what it covers
  • Keep the old host active until DNS has propagated and the new site has been tested
  • Check SSL, redirects, contact forms and email authentication after the move
  • Migrate business email separately; moving a website does not automatically move mailboxes

Expect DNS changes to take time to propagate. Do not cancel the old plan on the day you switch: keep it for at least a couple of weeks so you can compare uptime and fix anything that was missed.
