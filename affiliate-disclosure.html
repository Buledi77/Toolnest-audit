Disclosure

Which links on ToolNest are affiliate links, how they are marked, and what that does not change about our reviews.

Disclosure: Some links on ToolNest are affiliate links. If you click one and then buy or sign up, ToolNest may receive a commission at no extra cost to you. This affiliate revenue helps fund ToolNest. Commissions do not influence our listings, rankings or recommendations.

What this means for our content

  • We list cons and who each tool is not best for on every review.
  • Links that may earn us a commission are marked as sponsored in the page code and a notice appears near the top of each page.

Where affiliate links appear

Buttons such as "Try Tool" on tool pages, comparisons, alternatives pages and best-tools lists may be affiliate links. Some tools do not have an affiliate programme with us, and those buttons simply open the official site.

Editorial independence

Our editorial choices are ours alone, and nobody can pay to be listed, ranked higher or reviewed more kindly. If we ever receive a sponsored placement or a free account from a company, we will say so on that page.
