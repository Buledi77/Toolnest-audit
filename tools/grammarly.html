Grammarly overview

Grammarly checks what you write as you type and suggests fixes for spelling, grammar, clarity and tone. It works in a browser extension, desktop app and many everyday apps, so the help follows you from email to documents.

Why we recommend it

It improves the writing you already do every day, in the apps you already use, with almost no learning curve.

Who Grammarly is best for, and who should skip it

Best for

  • Anyone writing English emails, proposals or posts
  • Non-native English writers who want quick corrections
  • People who write in many different apps

Not best for

  • Writers who want long in-depth style reports on a manuscript
  • People who want a tool to write whole articles from nothing
  • Anyone who is not comfortable with a tool reading their text to check it

Grammarly pros and cons

Pros

  • Fixes mistakes as you type
  • Works in many apps
  • Helps with polite tone

Cons

  • Suggestions can be wrong, so you still decide
  • Some advanced features need a paid plan
  • It checks text but does not plan or research for you
