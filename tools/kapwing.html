Kapwing review: fast social video editing in a browser

Kapwing is a browser-based editor for people who need captioned, resized social video quickly, not a frame-accurate production suite. Subtitles, repurposing and AI cleanup are the draw; the credit system and browser constraints are the trade-off.

Kapwing overview

Kapwing runs in the browser and concentrates on the tasks that slow social teams down: transcribing and captioning clips, resizing for each platform, cutting silences, translating subtitles and repurposing long videos into shorts. It also includes a conventional timeline editor, screen recording and a stock library.

Why it stands out

The workflow is designed around output, not editing craft. You can upload a long video, generate captions, pick short segments and export vertical clips without installing software or learning a professional NLE.

Who Kapwing is best for, and who should skip it

Best for

  • Social media teams producing captioned clips at volume
  • Podcasters and creators repurposing long episodes into shorts
  • Marketers who need fast resizing and subtitle translation
  • Anyone editing on a Chromebook or a machine without desktop software
  • Small teams that want shared workspaces and brand kits

Skip it if

  • You need frame-accurate, multi-track professional editing; a desktop NLE is better
  • Your projects are long and effect-heavy; browser performance becomes a constraint
  • You need advanced colour grading, audio mixing or VFX
  • You cannot accept watermarked exports or a 720p free tier
  • You need offline editing

Choose neither if…

  • You need frame-accurate, multi-track editing as your main workflow
  • You need offline editing or raw footage handling at production scale
  • You only need to trim a clip once; a free desktop tool may be enough
  • You need advanced colour grading, audio mixing or visual effects

Kapwing pros and cons

Pros

  • Genuinely fast subtitling, including translation into many languages
  • Repurposing tools that turn one long video into multiple short clips
  • Runs in a browser with no installation, which suits Chromebooks and locked-down machines
  • The free plan is usable for evaluation and occasional short exports
  • Pro removes the watermark and adds 4K exports, brand kits and 200 GB of storage
  • Collaboration features make feedback and shared projects straightforward

Cons

  • The free plan is heavily limited: 720p, four-minute exports, a watermark and 30 minutes of export per month
  • Credits are consumed by AI features such as subtitling, dubbing and text to speech, and Pro's 1,000 credits can run out during heavy months
  • Browser editing can lag on long or complex projects
  • Not a replacement for professional editing software
  • Some advanced features (voice cloning, lip sync) require the Business plan
  • Pricing is shown with annual billing; monthly costs more

Features that matter in practice

Auto-subtitles

Generates captions in the browser, with translation into multiple languages on paid plans.

Repurposing

Finds highlights and creates short clips from long videos for social platforms.

Resize and templates

Shared workspaces, custom fonts and brand assets on paid plans.

Free plan and trial

Yes. The Free plan includes 10 credits, 720p exports, videos up to four minutes, a Kapwing watermark, 5 GB of storage and 30 minutes of export per month. It is enough to test the editor and produce occasional short clips, not to run a publishing schedule. No free trial of a paid plan is advertised; the Free plan is the evaluation route.

Exporting work and switching editors

Kapwing stores projects in its own workspace, so treat export as part of every finished job rather than something to arrange at the end of a subscription.

  • Download final MP4 files and any captions you need in a portable format
  • Check which project or timeline formats Kapwing can export before assuming another editor will open them
  • Keep original footage and audio outside Kapwing so a project can be rebuilt elsewhere
  • Download brand assets, fonts and templates you created before cancelling
  • Rebuild active projects in the new editor and compare export quality before switching the whole team

If you move to a desktop editor, expect to relink media and recreate captions and effects. Budget more time for long projects, and keep the Kapwing account active until the replacement has delivered at least one finished video.

How it compares and what to try instead

Kapwing is the fastest route from a long recording to captioned social clips. It is not the tool for frame-accurate editing, and it does not pretend to be.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kapwing free?

Kapwing has a free plan with 10 credits, 720p exports, a four-minute video limit, a watermark and 30 minutes of export per month. Paid plans remove the watermark and raise the limits.

How much does Kapwing cost?

Pro is $16 per month and Business is $50 per month with annual billing, as displayed in November 2026. Monthly billing costs more, and Enterprise is custom.

Is Kapwing good for YouTube?

For Shorts, captioned clips and repurposed segments, yes. For long-form, effect-heavy editing, a desktop editor gives you more control and better performance.

Does Kapwing add a watermark?

Yes on the Free plan. Pro and Business exports are watermark-free.
