Descript overview

Descript turns your recording into a transcript, and you edit the audio or video by editing the words. Delete a sentence in the text and it disappears from the recording, which makes editing feel like writing.

Why we recommend it

It makes editing speech-led video and podcasts as easy as editing a document, which is the biggest barrier for beginners.

Who Descript is best for, and who should skip it

Best for

  • Podcasters and YouTubers who talk to the camera or microphone
  • Beginners who find timeline editors confusing
  • Teams that want to cut a recording quickly from a transcript

Not best for

  • Filmmakers who need advanced colour grading and effects
  • Projects with little or no speech
  • Anyone who wants only a voiceover generator

Descript pros and cons

Pros

  • Edit by editing the text
  • Automatic transcripts
  • Removes filler words fast

Cons

  • Not a replacement for a full professional video suite
  • Transcript quality depends on audio quality
  • Some features depend on your plan
