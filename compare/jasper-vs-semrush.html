Jasper vs Semrush: which should you choose?

Jasper and Semrush solve two halves of content marketing. Semrush shows what people search for and what competitors rank for; Jasper helps you write the copy.

The short answer

Quick verdict

Pick Semrush when the problem is knowing what to write about, and Jasper when the problem is writing it quickly. Content teams often use research first and drafting second.

Side by side

FeatureJasperSemrush
Main jobDraft marketing copyFind keywords and check competitors
Best forAd copy and product page writingFinding keywords and checking competitors
DataWriting help, not search dataSearch and competitor data; figures are estimates
What you getDrafts to editKeyword lists, competitor reports and site audits
Learning curveTemplates make it easy to startMany features, so it takes time to learn
Watch out forDrafts need a human edit and fact checkPaid plans cost more than basic keyword tools

Which one should you choose?

Choose Jasper if

  • You already know what to write and need to write it faster
  • You produce ads and product pages often
  • You want a consistent brand voice

About each tool

Jasper

Marketing

Jasper is an AI writing platform aimed at marketing teams. It helps you draft ads, landing pages, product descriptions and campaign copy, and it can learn a brand voice so the text sounds consistent.
