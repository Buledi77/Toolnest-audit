Hostinger vs Systeme.io: which should you choose?

Hostinger hosts and builds a website you own. Systeme.io is an all-in-one platform for selling, with pages, email and checkout built in.

The short answer

Quick verdict

Pick Hostinger when the website itself is the goal, and Systeme.io when the sale is the goal. Some people use both: a site on Hostinger and a sales funnel on Systeme.io. Plans and limits change, so check both official sites.

Which one should you choose?

Choose Hostinger if

  • You want your own website and domain
  • You want room to grow, including WordPress
  • You need business email on your own domain

Choose Systeme.io if

  • You are selling a product, course or email list
  • You want sales pages, email and checkout in one account
  • You would rather not manage hosting at all

About each tool

Hostinger

Hosting

Hostinger is a web hosting company. You can use it to host a website, build one with its website builder, register a domain and set up business email from one account. It offers shared, WordPress, cloud and VPS hosting, so you can start small and move up later.

Systeme.io

Sales

Systeme.io is an all-in-one platform for selling online. It combines sales pages, an email list, checkout, online courses and automation, so you do not have to connect several separate tools.
