Head-to-head comparison

IFTTT vs Zapier

IFTTT is the cheaper way to connect consumer apps and smart devices. Zapier is the more capable business automation platform. The price gap is real, but so is the gap in multi-step logic, app coverage and support.

The short answer

Our verdict

IFTTT wins on price and simplicity. Zapier wins on workflow depth and business integrations. For a smart-home routine or a single social cross-post, paying for Zapier is unnecessary. For a lead-routing workflow with conditions, deduplication and a CRM, IFTTT will run out of room quickly.

Choose IFTTT if…

The automation is one trigger and one or two actions, runs on a schedule or a simple event, and does not need conditional branching or team permissions.

  • Your automations are simple trigger-to-action applets
  • You connect consumer apps, social accounts or smart-home devices
  • You want the lowest possible monthly cost
  • You do not need branching, filters at scale or team controls
Visit IFTTT (opens the official site in a new tab)

Choose Zapier if…

The automation is part of a business process: several steps, filters, error handling, shared ownership and an audit trail.

  • Workflows need multiple steps, filters, paths or webhooks
  • You connect business apps such as CRM, support or finance tools
  • You need shared workspaces, SSO and audit logs
  • Reliability and support matter more than the lowest price

Which workflow fits you

The pricing pages look further apart than the real cost of ownership. IFTTT's Starter plan is a few dollars a month; Zapier's Professional plan starts at $19.99 on annual billing. But Zapier's free tier allows only two-step Zaps and 100 tasks, while IFTTT's free tier allows unlimited Applets with 1,000 credits. At the low end, IFTTT is dramatically cheaper for the same amount of simple automation.

The comparison flips when a workflow gets complex. A Zap with a trigger, a filter, a formatter and two app actions can consume several tasks per run. IFTTT's credit model also charges per Applet run, and its AI features draw on the same allowance. The honest way to compare is to price one real workflow you run daily: count the runs per month, the number of steps, and the plan tier that covers both.

App coverage is the other deciding factor. Zapier's 9,000+ integrations include the long tail of business software: CRMs, helpdesks, billing tools, data warehouses and internal APIs via webhooks. IFTTT's 1,000+ services are excellent for Google, social networks, smart-home devices and consumer apps, but a niche B2B tool is often missing.

Finally, consider who owns the automation. IFTTT is built for personal use; Zapier is built for teams, with shared connections, folder permissions and SSO. If a workflow breaks while you are on holiday, that difference matters more than a few dollars a month.

Frequently asked questions

Is IFTTT cheaper than Zapier?

Yes. IFTTT's Starter plan is $2.09 per month billed annually, while Zapier's Professional plan starts at $19.99 per month billed annually. IFTTT's free tier is also more generous for simple Applets.

Can IFTTT run multi-step automations?

Yes, Applets can include multiple actions, queries, filters and delays. The limitation is the overall model: it is built around a trigger and its actions, not the branching, paths and code steps Zapier offers.

Does Zapier have a free plan?

Yes, with 100 tasks per month, two-step Zaps and 15-minute polling. It is enough to test the platform, not to run a business process.

Which is better for smart home automation?

IFTTT. Its consumer and device coverage is the reason it exists, and the price is far lower for that use case.
