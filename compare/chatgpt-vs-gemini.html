ChatGPT vs Gemini

Both have a free plan and several paid tiers, but they differ on price steps, bundled storage and how limits work. Here is how the official individual plans compare.

The short verdict

Quick verdict

Pick Gemini if you want the cheaper paid entry point, bundled Google storage, or a tight fit with Google's apps. Pick ChatGPT if you want OpenAI's own toolset, such as custom GPTs, projects, scheduled tasks and Codex, on a plan around $20 a month. If you are unsure, both have a free plan, so run the same real task in each.

ToolNest did not run benchmarks or hands-on tests for this page. The comparison rests on what the vendors say officially about their plans, so it tells you what each plan is documented to include, not which one gives better answers on your work.

Business, enterprise and API (not individual plans)

These are sold to organisations or developers, so they are kept out of the table above and should not be read as individual subscription prices.

ChatGPT (Business and Enterprise)

ChatGPT Business: $20 per user a month billed annually, or $25 billed monthly, minimum 2 users, per OpenAI's ChatGPT Work and Codex pricing documentation. ChatGPT Enterprise: enterprise agreement; OpenAI lists no price and says to contact sales. OpenAI says API token prices are separate from subscription usage.

Gemini (Workspace, Gemini Enterprise and API)

Google Workspace: Google's pricing page showed no dollar amounts when we read it. It lists Gemini in Workspace apps for the Standard, Plus and Enterprise tiers (limited on Starter), and caps Business plans at 300 users. Gemini Enterprise (a separate Google Cloud product): Business edition "Starting at $21 per seat per month" with a 30-day trial; Standard and Plus editions are sold through sales. The Gemini Developer API is priced per 1M tokens on a separate page and is not compared here.

Which one should you choose?

Choose ChatGPT if...

  • You want projects, scheduled tasks and custom GPTs. OpenAI lists all three on Plus.
  • You use Codex for coding. OpenAI lists limited Codex on Free and expanded Codex on Plus.
  • You want a path above Plus inside the same product. ChatGPT Pro offers three usage tiers (check current prices on OpenAI's pricing page).
  • You want to test deep research without paying. ChatGPT Free lists limited deep research, and Plus lists expanded deep research.

Choose Gemini if...

  • You want the cheapest paid plan. Google AI Plus is $4.99 a month in the US, against $8 for ChatGPT Go, and it includes 400 GB of storage.
  • You want cloud storage bundled with your AI plan. Google includes 15 GB on Free and up to 5 TB on AI Pro across Gmail, Drive and Photos.
  • You regularly work with very long inputs. Google documents a 1 million token context window on AI Pro and Ultra, the largest figure either company lists for its individual plans.
  • You live in Google's apps. Google's plan page lists "Access Gemini directly in Google apps" from AI Plus.

Trade-offs to know about

ChatGPT limitations

  • Limits vary by feature and plan, and OpenAI says limits apply even on paid tiers. Check the plan table before relying on any one capability.
  • Go may show ads, and Go is not available in every region.
  • Free is documented at 27K context for Instant models, which is small for long documents.
  • Pro comes in three usage tiers, and we could not confirm their consumer prices from ChatGPT's main pricing page.

Gemini limitations

  • Usage is a multiple of an unstated "standard" limit, so the absolute amount is not published, and Google says limits may change without notice.
  • Free is documented at a 32k context window and AI Plus at 128k, so the 1 million token window needs AI Pro.
  • Some features are limited to certain countries or tiers, and Google says Flow features vary by tier, platform and region.
  • The highest tier starts at $99.99 a month and is billed monthly only.
