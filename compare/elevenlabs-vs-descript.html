ElevenLabs vs Descript: which should you choose?

ElevenLabs creates a natural voiceover from text. Descript edits recordings you already have by editing the transcript. Many creators use both.

The short answer

Quick verdict

They are partners more than rivals. Use ElevenLabs when the voice is generated from text and Descript when the voice is yours. If you are unsure, start with the one that matches how you make content today.

Side by side

FeatureElevenLabsDescript
Main jobTurn text into natural speechEdit audio and video by editing the transcript
Starting pointA written scriptA recording of you talking
VoiceMany natural voices and languages; can clone a voice where the plan allowsUses your own recording; has voice tools on supported plans
EditingAudio generation, not a video editorFull audio and video editing for speech-led content
Best forNarration, courses and multi-language voiceoversPodcasts, talking-head videos and screen recordings
Watch out forVoices need listening to and tuning; read the rules on voice cloningTranscript quality depends on audio quality; not for heavy visual effects

Which one should you choose?

Choose ElevenLabs if

  • You want narration without recording yourself
  • You update lessons or scripts often
  • You need voiceovers in more than one language

Try ElevenLabs (opens the official site in a new tab)

Choose Descript if

  • You record yourself or guests
  • You want to cut a video by editing text
  • You want to remove filler words quickly

About each tool

ElevenLabs

Voice & Video

ElevenLabs turns written text into natural-sounding speech. You can narrate a video, a course or an audiobook without recording yourself, and many languages and voice styles are available.

Descript

Voice & Video

Descript turns your recording into a transcript, and you edit the audio or video by editing the words. Delete a sentence in the text and it disappears from the recording, which makes editing feel like writing.
