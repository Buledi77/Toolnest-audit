Claude vs Gemini

Both offer a free plan and tiered paid plans, but they differ on entry price, bundled storage and what each paid tier adds. Here is how the official individual plans compare.

The short verdict

Quick verdict

Pick Claude if your work centres on long documents, writing and code, and you want Claude Code, Projects and document-style outputs in one subscription. Pick Gemini if you want a cheaper paid entry plan, bundled Google storage, or a tight fit with Google's apps. If you are unsure, both have a free plan, so run the same real task in each.

ToolNest did not run benchmarks or hands-on tests for this page. The comparison rests on what the vendors say officially about their plans, so it tells you what each plan is documented to include, not which one gives better answers on your work.

Which one should you choose?

Choose Claude if...

  • You regularly work with very long documents, large codebases or long conversations. Anthropic says its newest paid-plan models reach 1M tokens, and Claude Code is included from Pro.
  • You want finished outputs such as Artifacts, documents, slides and designs. Anthropic lists Claude Design, Slides, Docs and Artifacts even on the Free plan.
  • You want Projects and scheduled or handed-off tasks. Anthropic lists both on Pro.
  • You work in Microsoft 365 or Chrome. Anthropic lists Claude in Chrome and Microsoft 365 as Pro features.

Choose Gemini if...

  • You want the cheapest paid plan. Google AI Plus is $4.99 a month in the US, while Claude's cheapest paid plan is Pro at $17 to $20 a month. Plus also includes 400 GB of storage.
  • You want cloud storage bundled with your AI plan. Google includes 15 GB on Free and up to 5 TB on AI Pro across Gmail, Drive and Photos.
  • You live in Google's apps. Google's plan page lists "Access Gemini directly in Google apps" from AI Plus.
  • You want image generation on a free plan. Google lists image generation and editing on Free, and Anthropic's pricing page does not mention image generation.

Trade-offs to know about

Claude limitations

  • Usage is rationed by a 5-hour window and a weekly limit, and long conversations, big attachments and heavy features use more of it. Anthropic may change limits to manage capacity.
  • Free does not include Opus or Fable, and on Pro, Fable runs on pay-as-you-go usage credits.
  • There is no plan between Free and Pro, so the entry price is $17 to $20 a month.
  • Anthropic's pricing page does not mention image generation, so confirm this in the app if it matters to you.

Gemini limitations

  • Usage is a multiple of an unstated "standard" limit, so the absolute amount is not published, and Google says limits may change without notice.
  • Free is documented at a 32k context window and AI Plus at 128k, so the 1 million token window needs AI Pro.
  • Some features are limited to certain countries or tiers, and Google says Flow features vary by tier, platform and region.
  • The highest tier starts at $99.99 a month and is billed monthly only.
