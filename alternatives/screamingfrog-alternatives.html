Screaming Frog alternatives: what you gain and what you lose

Screaming Frog is a specialist desktop crawler, so its alternatives are not equivalents. Cloud SEO suites add keyword data, rank tracking and scheduled audits, but none matches the crawl depth and extraction control of the £199 licence. The right switch depends on which limitation is actually hurting you.

Why people look for Screaming Frog alternatives

You need keyword research, backlink data or rank tracking in the same tool. Screaming Frog has none of those datasets.

You want scheduled cloud audits and shareable dashboards without running a desktop application or managing exports.

You need team access and client-facing reporting rather than CSV files.

You are auditing a site where a cloud crawl quota is sufficient and you do not need custom extraction or rendered HTML storage.

The shortlist: best Screaming Frog alternatives

Prices were checked in November 2026. Screaming Frog is priced in GBP per licence per year; the cloud suites are USD subscriptions with plan-based crawl quotas.

1

Semrush

SEO

The strongest cloud replacement if you need research plus scheduled audits.

What it does better: Keyword, competitor, backlink and paid-search research alongside Site Audit and Position Tracking, with client-ready reporting.

Trade-off: Crawl depth and extraction are more limited, and crawl quotas depend on your plan. You pay monthly rather than once a year.

Price context: SEO from $139/mo, or $117.33/mo billed yearly; limited free account.

2

Ahrefs

SEO

A research suite with a capable Site Audit attached.

What it does better: Large backlink and keyword datasets, Content Explorer and scheduled audits in one subscription.

Trade-off: Site Audit is less configurable than a desktop crawler, and the subscription costs far more per year.

Price context: Starter from $29/mo; Lite from $129/mo; free account with limits.

Read the Ahrefs review · Direct comparison
3

Moz Pro

SEO

The simplest bundle if you mainly need a crawl and rank tracking.

What it does better: A guided campaign model, rank tracking and keyword research alongside Site Crawl, at a lower entry price than Semrush or Ahrefs.

Trade-off: Smaller link index and a simpler crawler. Not suitable for very large or JavaScript-heavy technical audits.

Price context: Standard $99/mo, or $79/mo billed yearly.

What you give up when you switch

If you leave Screaming Frog for a cloud suite, you gain research data and scheduled reporting but lose crawl control. Specifically, you lose configurable rendering, custom extraction with XPath or regex, local crawl comparison and the ability to keep full crawl data on your own machine. For routine site hygiene that is a fair trade. For a migration or a 100,000-URL audit, it is usually a downgrade.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free Screaming Frog alternative?

Screaming Frog's own free version is the closest free equivalent, capped at 500 URLs per crawl. Cloud suites offer limited free accounts, but their audit quotas are smaller and less configurable.

Is Semrush a replacement for Screaming Frog?

For ongoing site monitoring and research, yes. For deep technical audits, migrations and custom extraction, no. Many teams keep both.

What is the best cloud alternative?

Semrush if you need the broadest data and reporting; Ahrefs if link and keyword research matters more; Moz Pro if you want the lowest-cost bundle with a crawl.

Can I use Screaming Frog for free forever?

The free version is not time-limited, but each crawl is capped at 500 URLs and advanced features are restricted. It is a permanent free tier, not a trial.
