Alternatives by use case

Best Moz Pro alternatives, chosen by the job you need

Moz Pro is a capable all-rounder, so people rarely leave because it is bad. They leave because one job needs more depth: backlink data, paid-search research, technical crawling or on-page content optimisation. Here are the four alternatives worth considering, and what each one costs you in return.

Why people look for Moz Pro alternatives

Moz Pro's backlink index is smaller than Ahrefs' or Semrush's. If link-gap analysis is a recurring deliverable, you will eventually hit domains Moz does not surface.

It has no serious paid-search or PPC research workflow. Marketers who need to compare organic and paid activity usually move to Semrush.

The cloud Site Crawl is simpler than a desktop crawler. Sites with tens of thousands of URLs, heavy JavaScript or migration comparisons need more control.

Content optimisation is basic. Teams that brief and score articles against search intent often add a dedicated content tool.

The shortlist: best Moz Pro alternatives

Prices were checked in November 2026 and are shown in USD unless stated. Surfer SEO's plans were not re-verified for this page, so no figure is quoted; confirm on its official site.

1

Semrush

SEO

The broadest replacement if Moz Pro feels too narrow.

What it does better: Competitor, keyword, backlink, paid-search, content and local research in one subscription, with stronger client reporting.

Trade-off: Higher entry price and a steeper interface. You will pay for modules you may not use.

Price context: SEO from $139/mo, or $117.33/mo billed yearly; limited free account.

2

Ahrefs

SEO

The best swap if backlinks and content research are the real job.

What it does better: A larger backlink index, stronger bulk keyword filtering and Content Explorer for editorial planning.

Trade-off: More expensive at the professional tiers and less forgiving for beginners. The cheapest Starter plan is limited.

Price context: Starter from $29/mo; Lite from $129/mo; free account with limits.

Read the Ahrefs review · Direct comparison
3

Screaming Frog

SEO

The specialist option when the crawl is the deliverable.

What it does better: Unlimited crawl size, JavaScript rendering, custom extraction and staging-versus-production comparisons for £199 a year.

Trade-off: No keyword research, backlink data or rank tracking. It complements a suite rather than replacing one.

Price context: £199 per licence per year; free version crawls 500 URLs.

4

Surfer SEO

SEO

For teams that need help writing and scoring pages, not just auditing them.

What it does better: On-page content scoring, outlines and SERP-driven suggestions inside a dedicated editor.

Trade-off: Not a replacement for rank tracking or a full site audit. It solves one part of the workflow.

Price context: Paid plans with a trial; check current pricing on Surfer's site.

Semrush — Broadest coverage, especially for agencies with multiple clients.

Large or JavaScript-heavy technical audit

Screaming Frog — Crawl depth and rendering control that a cloud suite cannot match.

Lowest-cost like-for-like replacement

Stay with Moz Pro — The Standard plan is cheaper than Semrush SEO or Ahrefs Lite.

What you give up when you switch

Switching rarely means one clean replacement. Moz Pro bundles several jobs, so replacing it with a specialist usually means adding a second subscription. If you move to Screaming Frog for crawling, you still need keyword and rank data. If you move to Ahrefs for links, you may still want a cheaper rank tracker. Budget for the combination, not the single tool.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free Moz Pro alternative?

Ahrefs offers a free account with limits, and Screaming Frog's free version crawls up to 500 URLs. Neither covers ongoing rank tracking and research at professional volume, so they are evaluation routes rather than permanent replacements.

Is Ahrefs better than Moz Pro?

For backlink and content research, yes. For a simple, lower-cost rank-tracking and audit workflow, Moz Pro is often the better fit. The switch only makes sense if link data changes your decisions.

What is the cheapest way to replace Moz Pro?

Screaming Frog's free tier plus a free keyword tool covers a small site. For professional use, Moz Pro Standard is already one of the cheaper all-in-one plans, so leaving usually costs more, not less.

Do I need Semrush and Screaming Frog?

Only if you need both market research and deep technical audits. Many small teams use Semrush or Moz Pro for research and the free Screaming Frog tier for occasional technical checks.
