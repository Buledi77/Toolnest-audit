Alternatives by use case

Best IFTTT alternatives, by the limit you hit

IFTTT is cheap and approachable, and most people leave only when a specific limit appears: a workflow needs branching, a business app is missing, polling is too slow, or the team needs controls. Here is which alternative solves each problem, and what it costs.

Why people look for IFTTT alternatives

The Applet model is trigger-and-action. Once a workflow needs conditional branches, loops or data transformation, you are chaining Applets and fighting the model.

Business SaaS coverage is thinner than Zapier's. A niche CRM, helpdesk or finance tool may simply not be available.

Polling is hourly on the free plan, 15 minutes on Starter and five minutes on Pro. If an event needs a near-immediate response, that is not enough.

Team governance is limited. There is no equivalent of shared workspaces, SSO and audit logs on the cheaper plans.

The shortlist: best IFTTT alternatives

Prices were checked in November 2026. IFTTT and Make figures are annual-billed monthly equivalents in USD; n8n Cloud is priced in EUR. Confirm current plans before buying.

1

Zapier

Automation

The broadest replacement for business app coverage.

What it does better: 9,000+ integrations, multi-step Zaps, filters, paths, webhooks, code steps and team controls.

Trade-off: Much higher entry price and task-based billing that multiplies with each step. Overkill for simple personal recipes.

Price context: Free 100 tasks/mo; Professional from $19.99/mo billed annually for 750 tasks.

2

Make

Automation

The visual builder for workflows that branch.

What it does better: A scenario canvas with routers, filters, iterators and error handlers, plus a lower cost per operation than Zapier.

Trade-off: Steeper learning curve and only two active scenarios on the free plan. Credit consumption per module can surprise you.

Price context: Free 1,000 credits/mo; Core $9/mo billed annually for 10,000 credits.

Read the Make review · Direct comparison
3

n8n

Automation

For technical teams that want to self-host and write code.

What it does better: Free Community Edition, unlimited steps per execution, code nodes, HTTP requests and data control on your own infrastructure.

Trade-off: You own upgrades, backups and uptime unless you pay for n8n Cloud. Assumes technical confidence.

Price context: Community Edition free self-hosted; Cloud from €20/mo billed annually.

What you give up when you switch

Every alternative trades IFTTT's simplicity for capability. Zapier adds apps and polish at a much higher price. Make adds workflow logic but asks you to learn a canvas and a credit model. n8n adds control but makes you responsible for infrastructure. If your Applet works today, the cheapest alternative is usually to keep it and add a second tool only for the workflow that outgrew it.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free IFTTT alternative?

Zapier's free plan allows 100 tasks per month and two-step Zaps; Make's allows 1,000 credits but only two active scenarios; n8n's Community Edition is free to self-host. IFTTT's own free tier remains the most usable for simple personal automation.

Can Zapier do everything IFTTT does?

Mostly, but at a higher price. IFTTT's consumer and smart-home coverage is broader in some areas, and its free tier is more generous for simple Applets.

Which alternative is cheapest for multi-step workflows?

Make at $9 per month billed annually is the cheapest hosted option for branching workflows. Self-hosted n8n has no licence fee but adds infrastructure and maintenance costs.

Do I need to migrate my Applets?

No. You can keep IFTTT for simple recipes and add another platform for the workflow that needs more logic. Migrating everything is rarely worth the effort.
