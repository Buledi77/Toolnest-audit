Best Writing Tools

Writing help comes in three kinds: correction, drafting and organisation. These picks cover each job with one clear tool.

How we picked

Correction, drafting and organisation are different jobs, so this list is ordered by how widely each job applies. These are our picks from the tools ToolNest currently covers, not every writing tool on the market.

  • Improves clarity, not just correctness.
  • Fits where you write.
  • Keeps your own voice.
  • Honest about when a human should review.

Our picks

1

Grammarly

Writing

Fixes mistakes and tone as you type in the apps you already use.

Best for
Everyday emails, proposals and posts
Skip it if
You want long in-depth style reports.
Why it ranks here
Correction helps almost every writer, and it works as you type with little setup.
Better option in this list
Jasper, if you draft marketing copy regularly.

2

Jasper

Marketing

Marketing templates and brand voice for ads and product pages.

Best for
Marketers and small businesses
Skip it if
You only write occasionally.
Why it ranks here
Drafting marketing copy is a narrower job, though it suits marketers well.
Better option in this list
Grammarly, if you write occasionally or mainly edit your own text.
Try Jasper (opens the official site in a new tab)Read the Jasper review →
3

Notion

Productivity

A workspace to draft, organise and plan writing, with optional AI help on some plans.

Best for
People who plan and draft in one place
Skip it if
You want a dedicated proofreader.
Why it ranks here
It organises writing rather than improving sentences, so it supports the other two tools instead of replacing them.
Better option in this list
Grammarly, if what you need is a proofreader.
